# SICECO Côte-d'Or official check

- SICECO Côte-d'Or current published public tariff is machine-verified on the regional official tariff page.
- Normal charge up to 22 kVA: 1.50 EUR per 12-hour period + 0.40 EUR/kWh + 0.005 EUR/minute.
- Rapid charge up to 50 kVA: 2.00 EUR per 12-hour period + 0.50 EUR/kWh + 0.025 EUR/minute.
- After more than 12 hours connected, a new billing session starts and a new fixed fee is charged.
- A 10 EUR fee is published for payment-procedure failure when more than 1 kWh was charged.
- Freshmile/eMSP retail pricing is not assumed to equal the SICECO published network tariff.
