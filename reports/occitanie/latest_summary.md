# Occitanie coverage

All 13 departments are accounted for. Révéo is preserved as the interdepartmental family owned by ten departmental energy syndicates, without inventing a single current regional tariff. Exact local/direct rules are separately modeled for Hérault Énergies, SDEHG/Freshmile, Alizé Toulouse, TE32/Freshmile and Montpellier Métropole/e-Totem. AQUÍ in Pyrénées-Orientales and the current 2026 confirmation of SDE82 pricing remain reference-only until an official direct price is resolved.
