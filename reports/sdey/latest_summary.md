# SDEY official check

- SDEY Yonne is currently operated by Electromaps, which replaced Freshmile.
- Direct public tariffs: <25 kW 0.40 EUR/kWh, >25 kW 0.50 EUR/kWh, >100 kW ultra-rapid 0.60 EUR/kWh.
- When connected without consumption: 15 minutes free, then 0.30 / 0.40 / 0.50 EUR per minute by tariff tier.
- Optional no-commitment subscription: 2.99 EUR/month, with 0.35 / 0.40 / 0.50 EUR/kWh subscriber prices.
- QR, Electromaps app and Electromaps BFC RFID are supported; SDEY currently states that bank-card payment is unavailable on its network.
- Third-party roaming/operator pricing may add a commission and remains separate from SDEY direct tariffs.
