# Connect&go — Moselle et Madon

Official local tariff validated from the network's own Connect&go site. The offer combines kWh + time pricing, day/night windows, a 30 kW threshold, optional 3 EUR/month subscription, and idle surcharges that apply without consumption. The network page announces 24 stations across 14 municipalities with 22/25/120 kW equipment. Freshmile is the access/service partner. Keep roaming tariffs separate.
