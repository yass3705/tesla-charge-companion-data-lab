# eCharge54 / SDE54

Current public eCharge54 tariff page validated with SDE54 authority and operator dataset context. The network has multiple local tariff groups: CD54 at 0.20 EUR/kWh AC up to 22 kW and 0.40 EUR/kWh DC above 25 kW; a 0.50 EUR/30-minute AC group for CCCPH/CCMM/CCPCST/Heillecourt/Nancy/T2L; and a mixed 0.40 EUR/kWh + 0.07 EUR per started minute after 4 hours (08:00-20:00 only) group for the listed SDE54 local scopes. Roaming base prices are published the same but eMSP service fees may be added. Station-to-group mapping is mandatory before ranking.
