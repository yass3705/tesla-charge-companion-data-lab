# SDED 52 — Haute-Marne

Official tariffs effective 1 August 2026 validated. Haut-Marnais using a Freshmile badge pay 0.39 EUR/kWh AC and 0.49 EUR/kWh DC; the SDED52 page publishes 0.49/0.59 EUR/kWh for other charging operators. Between 08:00 and 21:00, an idle fee of 2 EUR per 15-minute block applies without energy consumption after 2h30 AC or 1h DC and continues while connected. The block billing is preserved exactly.
