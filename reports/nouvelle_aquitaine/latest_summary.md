# Nouvelle-Aquitaine coverage

All 12 departments are accounted for. MObiVE covers 10 departments with a common 2025 grid except Pyrénées-Atlantiques (64), which still exposes its separate 2023 grid. Deux-Sèvres is covered by AlterBase and Vienne by Sorégies Mobilités. Distinct local public offers for Bordeaux Métropole, Limoges Métropole and La Rochelle are also preserved separately. The current exact municipal La Rochelle kWh price remains reference-only until a current deliberation is reconfirmed.
